About Chandran

I am Ar. Chandran Shanmugam, a registered Architect and Technical Delivery Specialist with 8+ years of professional experience leading complex project delivery, building envelope detailing, and digital delivery workflows. My career includes 3.5+ years in California at Studio Schicketanz (Sep 2022 – Apr 2026) managing architectural project development, Title 24/IBC compliance, and BIM automation, as well as 3 years at a reputed firm in Bangalore, India (2019 – 2022) focused on sustainable detailing and construction documentation.

As an Architect & Technical Delivery Specialist, I balance conceptual design thinking with rigorous technical execution.
